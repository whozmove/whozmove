# Team Board

When a session leads a team of other sessions for you and keeps a [team file](../reference/team.md), **Up next**
shows beside the list every session working now and what waits, in the team's order, by goal. Team Board shows every
job of that team: what's moving, who's on what, the goals and what's done this week. Every job either lists says how
pressing it is (Critical, P0, P1 or P2), on its line, its card and who's on what: Critical and P0 in the colour of
something to do now, P2 quieter. Both only read the team file; the session running the team keeps it.

When the team file names the session you talk to apart from the one running the team, that session is the chief here
too: Up next is at the end of its line, the heads name it ("shop · chief Iris"), and its row says "Chief" while it
works. The one running the team shows its name alone there. It still starts the queued jobs ("Up next · Atlas starts
it"), and Take Me There opens it. While the session you talk to isn't in the list, Up next is at the end of the line of
the one running the team.

## Opening it

**Up next**, at the end of the chief's line ([With a team](the-list.md#with-a-team)), opens Up next on All goals,
whenever a session is a team's chief.

Teams are for people working on Whozmove until anyone can make one: Up next, like every team surface, shows only while
**Show Teams** is ticked, in Working on Whozmove ▸ (hold ⌥ as you open the pawn menu), and the rest of Team Board only
while **Show Team Board** is ticked there too. Ticked:

- **Team Board**, in place of Up next at the end of the chief's line, opens Glance; a goal's tile there opens that
  goal's Up next.
- **Team Board** under Tools on the panel's back (four squares in the header), while a session is a team's chief: its
  page says how many of the team's jobs are moving (never one waiting its turn) and how many need you, and **Open Team
  Board** opens it on the first team, by its project's folder, A to Z.

It opens beside the list, as Storage's map does. Its ×, Esc once you've clicked in it, or what opened it pressed again,
closes it. Show Team Board unticked while Glance is open turns it to Up next's All goals.

## Up next

Its head says **Up next**, with the project's folder and its chief's name ("shop · chief Atlas"). Under it, the gear's
own line with how many are working, as Up next has no seats to light ("10 working · 2 over the gear"), and why, then,
except while the session is at its limit (when the card hides its seats), a line saying where to change your gear: "Change your gear on the usage card, under
the panel: press a seat." With no gear set yet, that line says "No gear yet: press a seat on the usage card to let
jobs in.", and nothing is let in. With Usage Gear off in Settings there's no gear's line, and in its place "Usage Gear
is off in Settings, so there's no limit on what starts.": every queued job is let in. Then how many are shown: "All goals · 4 working · 3 up next" ("Spring launch · 1 working · 2 up
next" in one goal). While Show Team Board is ticked, ‹ goes back to Glance.

- **The goals down the side:** All goals first, then each goal in the file's order, numbered, then a heading the file
  gives for how the team works, then **Other**, for jobs under no goal, when there are some. Each says how many
  sessions are working on its jobs ("2 working"). A team file without goals has no list down the side.
- **All goals:** every session working now, by when its turn began, each with its goal's number ("–" for none), its
  name and what it's on: a member's job, "Chief" for the chief, "Your session" for one not in the team; and
  "Moving · Build", its job's step. Then the team's queued jobs the gear lets in, reading "Up next · Atlas starts it";
  then the gear's line, its number at the left; then the sessions working beyond your gear, saying "Over the gear ·
  carries on", as the gear holds back only queued jobs waiting to start; then the rest of the team's queued jobs,
  "Up next". Queued jobs go in the team's order, the most pressing first, then the one queued first, each with how
  pressing it is.
- **Let in, not started:** a job the gear let in that's still queued 10 minutes later reads "Not started: Atlas starts
  it", with **Take Me There** beside it, which opens the session running the team ("Open Atlas Again" when it has
  stopped; why not, when it isn't in the list).
- **More than one team led:** the gear's room is one for your account, so the jobs let in are the first that fit of
  every led team's queued jobs together, in the same order; each team's Up next marks only its own.
- **One goal:** its own sessions working and its queued jobs, the same way, with no line, as the gear counts every
  goal together: a job here that's next to start says so on its row. The chief and sessions with no job of the team's
  show only under All goals.
- Pressing a job opens its card at the foot, as on Glance; pressing the chief's row, or a session with no job of the
  team's, brings that session forward, as a row in the list does.

What waits on you, what's on hold and what's done aren't listed: their rows in the list say what they need.

Rows follow the sessions as they start and finish their turns: one that goes fades; the rest slide once, straight to
where they end up; one that comes fades in. Your gear is changed in one place, the usage card's seats
([The usage card](usage.md#your-gear)). Press one while Up next shows and the card's number turns first, then the line
leaves its gap and draws in at its new one, then each row it passed says its new words, one after another; every row
keeps the line's room under it, so no row moves. With reduced motion, the rows going and coming cross-fade, the new
words are simply there, and nothing slides.

| When | Up next says |
|---|---|
| Nothing waits | Nothing is up next. Atlas's queued jobs show here, the most pressing first. |
| No one working, nothing waits | No one is working, and nothing is up next. Sessions working show here, then Atlas's queued jobs, the most pressing first. |
| A goal with nothing working or waiting | Nothing in this goal is working or up next: Atlas puts its jobs here. |
| Under the rows, on All goals | Every session working now, then what waits, in Atlas's order. The gear's line shows only here, on All goals, because the gear counts every goal together. |
| Under the rows, in one goal | This goal's jobs, in Atlas's order. The gear's line is on All goals, because the gear counts every goal together. A job here that's next to start says so on its row. |
| No gear set yet | no line and nothing "starts it"; the gear's own line says why, as on the usage card |
| At the session's limit | the line stays, and nothing reads "starts it" |
| A long name or title | it ends with "…"; a job's card shows the whole of it |

VoiceOver reads each goal's number as "Goal 1", "–" as "No goal", and the line as "Gear 10: the first 10 work, the
rest wait".

## Glance

Its head says **Team Board**, with the project's folder and its chief's name under it.

The board shows the team's work and answers nothing: what needs you is answered in [One Task](one-task.md).

- **What needs you**, one quiet line: how many of the team's jobs wait on you ("5 need you"), each kind's mark with how
  many (Try, Pick, Approve and the rest, as One Task names them; point at a mark to read it, "1 Try"), and **One Task
  ›**, which opens it. When the session running the team has sent One Task fewer of them than wait, it says how many are
  there ("1 in One Task ›"): One Task shows only what's been sent it. With nothing waiting it says "✓ Nothing needs
  you".
- **What's moving**, the one big number, and beside it how many jobs are up next, waiting their turn (when any) and on
  hold. A job at a step that waits its turn, with nobody working on it ("Ready to try"), counts as waiting, not moving,
  and is said by what it waits for: "6 ready to try" while every one waits at that step, else "6 waiting their
  turn".
- **Stages**, a strip of chips: one for each step the team's jobs under way are at, in the order of their steps, with
  how many are there ("Ready to try 6"). The first 12 show, then **Show All**. Press one to see its jobs under the
  strip, the most pressing first, then the smallest, then the one that takes the least work, then the one that's been
  there longest, as a line under them says: the first 12, then **Show All**. A job the team file doesn't size, or whose
  work it doesn't say, counts as medium. Press the chip again to fold them; a stage that empties folds by itself. A long
  step's name ends with "…". With no job at a step, there's no strip. Queued, on hold and done jobs keep their own
  places.
- **Who's on what**: a line for each member with a job under way, in the order of their first job: its name and what
  it looks after, the job its row in the list names now with that job's goal number, its step and the step's track,
  then "2 need you", "+1 moving" and "3 up next" for its other jobs, each only when there are some, and how long since
  the job got where it is. The first 12 show, then **Show All**. Press a member to see its other jobs under it, by
  where they stand; press it again to fold them. A job waiting its turn has no one at work on it: it's among its
  member's other jobs, not what the member is on. A member with no other jobs has nothing to press. With no one on a
  job, it says "No one's on a job now."
- **Goals**, a tile each, three across, in the order the file gives and numbered by it: its name, when it's due, a bar
  of its jobs by where they stand, and how many need you (when any), are moving and are done, of how many. A heading the
  file gives for how the team works ("How we work") has its tile after the goals, with no number. Jobs under no goal
  have a last tile, "Other"; without goals in the file, one tile holds every job, "All tasks". A goal with no
  jobs says "No jobs yet". A tile opens that goal's Up next. Jobs waiting their turn show in its bar, grey.
- **Done this week**, the one done last first: 5, then **Show All**. Left out when nothing was done this week.

Where a job stands has the colour its row in the list has: at work cream, your move amber (red for a Critical or P0
job, as its ask is in the list), done green, up next, waiting and on hold grey. A job waiting its turn says its step's word ("Ready to try") on its line and its card.

With more than one team, the head has a button for each, by its project's folder.

## A job's card

Pressing a line opens its card at the foot: where it is and for how long, whose it is and what that member looks after,
its title, its steps as one bar the card's width (none for a job of one step), what it wants of you in full, what it
holds up, and **Take Me There**, which brings its member's session forward: after you clear its conversation, or open a
new one in the member's workspace, it's that one while it runs, never a duplicate. One that has stopped says so, and its
button, **Open Wren Again**, carries it on, as pressing its ended row does. A member that isn't in the list now: the card says "Wren isn't
in the list now: this opens Atlas", and the button opens the session running the team (or carries it on). A job nobody
has yet opens that session the same way. With neither in the list, the card says so, with no button, as there's nothing
listed to open.

## What it says when

| When | It says |
|---|---|
| The team file hasn't been read yet | Reading the team file… |
| The team has no jobs | Nothing on this team's board yet: its chief adds jobs to the team file |
| The team is no longer in the file | This team isn't in the team file any more |
| No one on a job | No one's on a job now. |
| Nothing waits on you | ✓ Nothing needs you |
| A chief without a name | "the chief", in place of its name |
| A session running the team without a name, behind a session you talk to | "the session running the team" |
| A long title | the line ends with "…"; the card shows the whole of it |

What Glance shows follows the team file: when the file changes, it's drawn again as it is, with no motion. Moving
between Glance, Up next and its goals fades in, as the panel's other views do.

VoiceOver reads each kind's mark by its word and how many, each stage's chip with its whole name ("Ready to try, 6
jobs"), and each tile as one line ("Goal 1, Spring launch: 1 needs you, 2 moving, 1 ready to try, 1 done, 5 jobs", what
waits its turn only when there are some, in the hero's words); the bars and dots are left out, as the words say it all.
