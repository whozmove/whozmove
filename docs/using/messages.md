# Messages

Claude Code's sessions can send each other messages. Messages shows every one your sessions sent in the last two days,
beside the list, in time order, like a channel: who sent it to whom, how pressing its sender said it was, its words,
and where it stands, read, answered, waiting, held or lost. It's for people working on Whozmove until anyone can make
a team: **Show Messages**, in Working on Whozmove ▸ (hold ⌥ as you open the pawn menu), opens it, and is ticked while
it shows. Its ×, or Show Messages pressed again, closes it.

## The inbox

When a session leads a team for you and keeps a [team file](../reference/team.md), the other sessions in its project
can write to its inbox rather than send it a message straight away: Whozmove keeps the message first, with how
pressing it is (urgent, question, report or note), then the session sends the lead a short line saying one waits. The
lead reads its inbox one message at a time, most pressing first, and marks each answered. So a message reaches the
lead even when the line telling it is held in its tab for your OK and expires unread. A lead that starts afresh reads
what waited for the one before. How a session writes, and what the lead's asks print, is in
[The inbox](../reference/inbox.md).

Above the channel, a strip shows each inbox with messages its lead hasn't read yet, in the order it will read them:
"Atlas's inbox · 2 to read, most pressing first". Pressing one shows that message.

## Each message

Its sender's name and its receiver's, its time, and for one kept in an inbox, its tag: **Urgent**, **Question**,
**Report** or **Note**. Then its words, drawn as the reader draws a plan, the first three lines and **Show All** for a
long one; "↳ answers Wren: …" when it answers one from the session it's sent to; and where it stands:

- **Answered by Atlas · 22:04**: the session it was sent to wrote back after it, or, for one in an inbox, the lead
  marked it answered. Pressing it shows the reply. Marked answered with no reply, it shows the lead's note.
- **Read by Atlas · 22:04**: read from the inbox, not answered yet.
- **In Atlas's inbox · not read yet · 6m**.
- **Kept in Atlas's inbox · Atlas's tab held the note telling it**, or the note expired, was turned down or didn't go:
  the message waits in the inbox all the same, so there's nothing for you to do.
- **Held in Wren's tab: your OK is on Wren's row**: a message sent straight to a session that runs in another mode
  waits there for you to let it in. Its row is the one place to do that; Messages only says where.
- **Held: Wren can't ask you to let it in**, as in Claude Desktop or a session in the background: nothing can let it in
  there, so it waits until it expires. **Copy** puts its words on the clipboard, to send again another way.
- **Expired unread: Wren never saw it**, **Turned down in Wren's tab**, **Dropped: Wren never got it** or **Didn't go:
  no session listens at that address now**, in the colour of something gone wrong, with **Copy**.
- **Sent · not seen in Wren's conversation yet**.

A message sent straight to a session that arrived and wasn't answered says nothing more, so the channel stays quiet.
Nothing in Messages is your move, so nothing in it is in that colour.

## Narrowing it

- **All**, **Waiting** (held, or kept in an inbox and not answered: a report or a note once it's read doesn't wait)
  and **Not Delivered** (sent straight to a session and never got there; one kept in an inbox never counts).
- Pressing a session's name shows everything it sent and received, from and to every session, with "Wren's messages
  ×" to go back to all; the strip then shows only its messages. Pressing the other name in one of them narrows it to
  those two. A session is told apart by itself, not its name: where two in the channel share a name, each says its
  folder, or "ended".
- The head counts this week's messages to a team's lead that went astray, when there were any: "This week: 3 held, 1
  lost".

With nothing to show, it says so: "Messages your Claude Code sessions send each other show here", "Reading the last
two days…" as Whozmove starts, or "Nothing from or to Wren in the last two days". While no agent on this Mac records
what its sessions send each other, an empty channel says "Nothing to show yet: for now, Messages shows only what's
written to a lead's inbox", and above the messages an inbox kept, "For now, only messages written to a lead's inbox
show here".
