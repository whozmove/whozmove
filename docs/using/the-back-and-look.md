# The panel's back, the menu bar and the look

Behind the list are Settings and the apps Whozmove works with, with their switches; in the menu bar, a count of the
sessions waiting on you and Whozmove's menu; and the panel's look, Grey, Black or Glass, light or dark, in Settings.

## The panel's back

The four squares in the header turn the whole panel over, and behind the list are **Settings** (below) and the apps
Whozmove works with, four across: Claude, Ghostty, iTerm2, Terminal, Spotify, Git and Jira (the terminals when they're on
your Mac), and under them, its own **Tools**: [Storage](storage.md), and [Team Board](team-board.md) while a session
leads a team and Working on Whozmove ▸ Show Team Board is ticked. Press one for its page:

- what Whozmove does with it;
- its switches: whether Claude Desktop's sessions are tracked, the terminal new conversations open in, Spotify's
  song, lyrics and icon, Git's next steps and Deploy, Storage's map and warning;
- how what Whozmove needs there stands, with its button: Whozmove's plugin on Claude's, with **Add**, **Turn On**,
  **Try Again** or **Get Claude Code**, and **Get Claude Desktop** on a Mac without it; Apple's command line developer
  tools on Git's page, with **Install**;
- **Sessions**, on each terminal's page and Claude's (below);
- what macOS lets Whozmove do with an app (Accessibility or Automation, with **Open System Settings** when it isn't
  allowed);
- and **Open**.

A tool's page leads with what it does, and says what macOS has to allow only once it's needed.

Claude's page is for Claude as a whole, Claude Code and Claude Desktop alike: with **Track Claude Desktop app
sessions** off, Claude Desktop's conversations leave the list, and new conversations start only in a terminal.

### Sessions

**Sessions**, with how many there are, lists every conversation Claude Code keeps on this Mac, wherever it ran, by
project, newest first. Each says when it ended and where it ran, or, while it's still open, where and when it last
replied, and whether it has a reply you haven't read (with a team file, a conversation that has ended in a team
member's folder never has: it's that member's, as in [With a team](the-list.md#with-a-team)).

Tick the ones you want gone (**Tick All Ended** ticks every one that has ended) and press **Move to Trash**. It asks
first, saying how many are still open and how many have replies you haven't read; then each goes to the Trash on its
own, an open one ended first (the question says so, as anything typed in it and not sent goes), and **Undo** brings them
back. **In the Trash** lists what you've moved, each with **Put Back** and **Show in Finder**, until you empty the
Trash; Finder's own **Put Back** works for any of its files too. Your typed prompts stay in Claude Code's history.

Some can't be ticked now, and say why, with the way past it: one that's working, waiting on you, or has agents or a
command still at work (**Take me there**), one paused in its terminal, open somewhere Whozmove can't end it, or the one
you're looking at. A conversation Claude Desktop keeps is never moved from here, whether its sessions are tracked or
not: it says **Delete it in Claude Desktop**, with **Open in Claude Desktop**.

The back is the panel's size, scrolling when there's more. ‹ at the header's left goes back a page; on the apps, it
turns the panel back to the list, its left edge sinking in. The rows, where the four squares were, turn the panel back
to the list too, from the right, and its chevron folds the panel away. An open reader folds back first, and ⌃⌥N turns
the panel back to start a conversation. Turned over again, it opens on your apps.

## Settings

**Settings**, first among the apps (or **Settings…** in Whozmove's menu, which goes straight there), is laid out as a
phone's: each row has its coloured tile, its name and what it's set to; press one for its page, and ‹ to come back.

- **Appearance**: the look (below);
- **Show Conversation Text**: a switch, on until you turn it off. Off, rows show no one's words: not yours, not what
  another session sent, not Claude's replies or what it's working on (see [The list](the-list.md#hiding-the-conversation));
- **Open Files With**: the editor a changed file opens in, from those on your Mac, each with its icon (see
  [Changes and next steps](changes-and-next-steps.md)), or **Get Visual Studio Code** when none of them is there;
- **New Conversation with ⌃⌥N**: a switch for ⌃⌥N in any app;
- **Bring Forward**: a session to bring forward from any app by holding two keys, and the keys (**Fn + Option** until
  you pick **Fn + Shift**). See [The list](the-list.md#bring-a-session-forward-with-two-keys);
- **Compact at Breaks**: a switch, off until you turn it on, for long conversations to be compacted for you at a
  natural break (see [Model, effort, mode and compacting](model-and-compact.md));
- **Usage Gear**: a switch, on until you turn it off, for your gear on the usage card. Off, queued jobs start without
  a limit, the card shows only your usage, no row says Over the gear, and the gear you set is forgotten (see [The
  usage card](usage.md#your-gear)).
- **Tickets**, once a project's way is set: where each project's tickets open, saying how many projects have one, each
  changed after its before and after (see [Tickets](tickets.md#where-each-projects-tickets-open)).

Some choices matter more than most: their heading says **Important**, and picking another shows what it changes
first, before and after, with **Keep It** and **Change**.

## The look

**Appearance**, in Settings: the panel **Grey** (until you pick otherwise), **Black** (**White** on a light panel), or
**Glass**, showing what's behind it, blurred, each shown as a small panel; and **Match the Mac**, **Light** or
**Dark**. The panel changes as you pick, and its cards, the usage card and Spotify's token all follow it. While Reduce
Transparency is on in System Settings, Glass is grey and can't be picked, with **Open Display Settings** under it.

## The menu bar

Whozmove's mark in the menu bar shows a count of sessions waiting on you. Its menu has what you need while the panel is
hidden, folded or lost off the screen:

- **New Conversation** (⌃⌥N);
- **Show / Hide Panel** and **Reset Position**;
- **Settings…**, the panel turned over straight to Settings;
- **Open at Login**, just above Quit (see [Installing](install.md));
- **Quit Whozmove**.
