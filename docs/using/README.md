# Using Whozmove

Everything the panel does, a page for each part of it.

- [The list](the-list.md): every session, newest first, whose move it is in each, and the row you're on.
- [Group by Team](group-by-team.md): the list by team, when a session leads a team of other sessions for you.
- [Team Board](team-board.md): Up next, every session working now and what waits, by goal; and a team's work: what's
  moving, who's on what, its goals and what's done this week.
- [Messages](messages.md): every message your sessions send each other, beside the list, and the inbox a team's lead
  reads one message at a time.
- [Requests, questions and plans](requests.md): answering Claude from its row.
- [One Task](one-task.md): the one thing that needs you most, beside the list, and talking with the agent that
  asked.
- [Side chat](side-chat.md): talking with a session beside the list while it works, your words reaching it between
  its steps.
- [The folded panel](folded-panel.md): what needs you, coming out beside it by itself.
- [What needs you](notifications.md): the line under the header, most urgent first, and what comes out beside the
  folded panel first.
- [Starting a conversation](new-conversations.md): the card, ⌃⌥N, projects and worktrees.
- [Tickets](tickets.md): giving a conversation a Jira ticket, and its row showing it.
- [Waiting on someone else](waiting.md): a session waiting on a review or a merge, holding no seat, and carrying on
  when it comes.
- [Model, effort, mode and compacting](model-and-compact.md): a session's settings from its row, and its context.
- [Changes and next steps](changes-and-next-steps.md): what isn't committed, and Commit, Push and Create PR.
- [Deploy](deploy.md): putting a checkout's code in place on your Mac with one press.
- [What a session wrote](reader.md): its plans, prototypes and notes, beside the list.
- [Plans at a glance](plans.md): a plan's short view, what you decide, and the whole plan folded.
- [Your plan's usage](usage.md): the session and the week, on a card under the panel.
- [Spotify and lyrics](spotify.md): the song playing, its controls and its lyrics.
- [Storage](storage.md): how full your disk is, a map of what fills it, and Clean up.
- [The panel's back, the menu bar and the look](the-back-and-look.md): Settings and the apps Whozmove works with, its
  menu, and Grey, Black or Glass, light or dark.
- [Installing](install.md): Homebrew, what it needs, setting itself up, permissions, building from source and
  uninstalling.

How Whozmove learns all this, source by source, is in [Where it reads from](../how-it-works/where-it-reads.md).
