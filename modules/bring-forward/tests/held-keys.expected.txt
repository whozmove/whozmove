== Saved picks, and when held keys count
A saved pick:
  The Chief, as its choice has always been saved: The Chief
  a session: that session
  a session named lead: that session
  another word: Off
  a session with no id: Off
  none: Off
Fn + Option:
  Fn, then Option, held half a second: at 0.35s
  Option, then Fn, held half a second: at 0.35s
  both, the right-hand Option: at 0.30s
  both, let go after 0.2s: never
  both, with Caps Lock on: at 0.30s
  both, with ⌘ held too: never
  both, down to them from ⌘ held too, as ⌘ is let go: never
  both, with ⌘ pressed and let go while they stay held: never
  both, with a key pressed after 0.1s (a shortcut, as ⌥fn⌫ is): never
  both, with a key pressed as they came down (an arrow, if it sets Fn's bit): never
  ⌘V pasted with fn held (a dictation app's paste): never
  both, held three seconds: at 0.30s
  both, let go, then held again: at 0.30s and 0.85s
  only Fn: never
  Fn + Control held instead: never
Fn + Shift:
  Fn, then Shift, held half a second: at 0.35s
  Shift, then Fn, held half a second: at 0.35s
  both, the right-hand Shift: at 0.30s
  both, let go after 0.2s: never
  both, with Caps Lock on: at 0.30s
  both, with ⌘ held too: never
  both, down to them from ⌘ held too, as ⌘ is let go: never
  both, with ⌘ pressed and let go while they stay held: never
  both, with a key pressed after 0.1s (a shortcut, as ⌥fn⌫ is): never
  both, with a key pressed as they came down (an arrow, if it sets Fn's bit): never
  ⌘V pasted with fn held (a dictation app's paste): never
  both, held three seconds: at 0.30s
  both, let go, then held again: at 0.30s and 0.85s
  only Fn: never
  Fn + Option held instead: never
